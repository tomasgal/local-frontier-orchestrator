# Project dissemination

This page tracks public and academic dissemination of the Human–AI research programme built around the Local Frontier Orchestrator, including *Balancing the Loop* and the Generic Epistemic Mediator workstream.

The items below are dissemination outputs and venues. They should not be read as evidence that the mediator has already demonstrated debiasing efficacy.

## Departmental dissemination

- **Cognitive Science Seminar, Comenius University Bratislava** — departmental seminar venue for presenting and discussing the project and its Human–AI interaction research direction:  
  https://cogsci.fmph.uniba.sk/seminar/

## Conference dissemination

- **Informačné interakcie — conference poster** — poster dissemination of the *Balancing the Loop* research programme, archived on Zenodo:  
  https://zenodo.org/records/24287930

## Relationship to reproducibility artifacts

Research dissemination is intentionally kept separate from runtime validation and software-release evidence.

- LFO software releases document reproducible source/runtime boundaries.
- Experiment artifacts document the exact apparatus, prompts, replay banks, mediator parameters and analysis choices used in a study.
- Seminar and conference outputs communicate the research programme and results to academic audiences.
- A dissemination output does not by itself establish causal efficacy or validate the Generic Epistemic Mediator.
